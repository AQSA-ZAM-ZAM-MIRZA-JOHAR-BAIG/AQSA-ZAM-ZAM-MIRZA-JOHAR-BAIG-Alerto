Flagship Project

Alerto — Intelligent Market Bot
Built by Aqsa Zam Zam Mirza Johar Baig

A production-grade Telegram bot that delivers real-time stock & crypto prices, smart price alerts, technical analysis, live watchlists, and AI-powered market insights — all within your Telegram chat.

Alerto is the flagship creation of Aqsa Zam Zam Mirza Johar Baig. It was designed and built entirely in Python using the python-telegram-bot async framework, backed by SQLite for persistent user data, and integrated with multiple live data providers including Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko.

The bot features a multi-phase architecture spanning six functional modules: real-time price lookups across global markets (US, NSE India, BSE India, and all crypto), a smart alerting engine that fires when prices breach user-defined thresholds, a live-streaming watchlist that updates every 10 seconds, deep technical analysis with RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, and candlestick pattern detection, fundamental analysis with key financial ratios, and scheduled hourly watchlist reports.

Alerto includes a built-in Telegram Web App for a richer visual watchlist experience, an intelligent symbol resolver that auto-detects whether a ticker is a US stock, Indian stock, or cryptocurrency, rate limiting, and admin-level controls.

What Alerto Can Do

Core Features of Alerto

Six powerful modules built by Aqsa Zam Zam Mirza Johar Baig to cover every aspect of market intelligence directly in Telegram.

📊

Real-Time Price Lookup

Instant stock prices from US markets (NYSE, NASDAQ), Indian markets (NSE & BSE), global indices (S&P 500, NIFTY 50), ETFs, and all cryptocurrencies. The smart symbol resolver auto-detects the correct market — just type the ticker.

/price AAPL /price RELIANCE /price ^GSPC /crypto BTC
🔔

Smart Price Alerts

Set intelligent price alerts with four trigger types: absolute price thresholds (above/below) and percentage-based movements (rise/drop). Alerto monitors prices every 30 seconds and sends you an instant Telegram notification the moment your condition is hit.

/setalert BTC above 100000 /setalert AAPL below 150 /setalert NIFTY drop 3 /alerts
📋

Live Portfolio Watchlist

Build a personal watchlist of up to 20 stocks and cryptocurrencies. View all your holdings with live prices in a single command. Activate Live Streaming mode to get a single message that auto-refreshes with real-time prices every 10 seconds — no manual refreshing needed.

/addstock TSLA /watchlist /livewatchlist /stoplive
📈

Technical Analysis & Charts

Deep technical analysis powered by 180 days of historical data. Calculates RSI, MACD (crossover detection), Bollinger Bands, identifies candlestick patterns, detects trend direction, finds key support & resistance levels, and generates an AI-powered market insight summary. ASCII sparkline charts for quick visual context.

/technical AAPL /chart ETH /technical TATAMOTORS.NS
🔍

Fundamental Analysis

Go beyond price with deep fundamental data. Alerto retrieves P/E ratio, EPS, market capitalization, revenue, profit margins, 52-week high/low, dividend yield, beta, and more — giving you the full picture before you make any investment decision.

/analyze AAPL /fundamental RELIANCE.NS /analyze GOOGL
📰

Hourly Reports & Symbol Search

Enable scheduled hourly watchlist reports to stay updated without checking manually. Use the intelligent search command to find any stock or crypto by company name — it searches across both equity and crypto markets simultaneously and returns ranked results with exchange info.

/hourly_report on /search apple /search bitcoin /search reliance
